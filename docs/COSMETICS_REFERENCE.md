# Knives - reference study and design notes (2026-11-17)

Direction (Fernando): each weapon looks like the real thing - shape, construction AND colours/finishes - with our
own details, never 1:1, never a shared "client" colour scheme. Proper, realistic finishes (Damascus, polish, real
handle materials, accessories), not a uniform neon look. Then custom animations, effects and particles per weapon.

## What makes the real ones look real (what we copy in spirit)
- **Damascus (pattern-welded)** is forged with the blade, then acid-etched: the layers run *with* the blade and
  look organic, like flowing water or smoke; a repeating, too-perfect pattern reads as fake/printed. Ladder and
  raindrop come from grooves/holes cut in the billet; twist from twisting the bar. -> our painter: layers along the
  blade face, low-frequency warp + fine lamination, varied layer width, crisp antialiased etch edges; the sharpened
  bevel shows the pattern at low contrast (polished back), never a stamp.
- **Stonewash** = tumbled with abrasive stones: an even matte grey, softly pebbled, scratch-hiding; reads as satin
  from a distance. -> low-contrast mottle + faint curved scuffs, no per-texel sparkle.
- **Satin** = parallel brush lines along the blade; **mirror** = reflects the room: sharp horizon, dark and bright
  bands. -> the dagger's mirror finish has one sharp horizon line, not a soft chrome gradient.
- **Edge**: the cutting edge is ground and sharpened separately from the flats -> every blade gets a bright fresh
  bevel, a crisp apex line and a darker shoulder (`materials.blade_finish`).
- **Karambit anatomy**: curved blade, sharp point, inside (concave) edge, outside edge, ergonomic handle; modern ones
  add the retention ring, a back edge near the tip, front/rear brakes (guards), a thumb rise with jimping.
- **Balisong anatomy**: tang + pivot pins, kicker, choil, swedge (unsharpened top bevel), safe/bite handles, latch
  on the bite handle, zen or tang pins; channel (one milled piece), sandwich (slabs + spacer) or chanwich handles.
- **Fairbairn-Sykes**: slender double-edged spear point with a centre ridge (~7"), short flat guard, vase-shaped
  ring grip (~4.9"), pommel nut; originally often brightly polished.

## Construction (v5, Fernando 2026-11-17: "looks spliced, like three images layered")
v4 built each knife from separate solids butted together with flat cuts (blade | wood | ring). Real knives are
assembled, so v5 builds them that way (`steel_loft` in knives.py):
- **Karambit**: ONE steel body - ring neck, full tang, ricasso with choil, blade - a single surface in one Damascus
  billet (the ring is cut from the same plate, flaring out of the neck with a fillet). The grind runs out at a
  plunge line mid-ricasso. Cocobolo scales are domed slabs inset from the steel outline over red G10 liners, with
  rounded, thinned ends, so a rim of the same steel shows all round the handle - like a real full-tang knife.
- **Balisong**: blade, ricasso, kicker, choil and tang are one stonewashed body that runs down BETWEEN the handles;
  the handle tops are domed round the pivot pins (no block stacked on the handles).
- **Dagger**: the blade's tang enters the guard through a flat ricasso and a raised boss with the slot's shadow; a
  steel ferrule sits between guard and grip; the brass nut sits on the tang's threaded end.

## Quality audit (offline, honest) - four passes
1. v1-v3 used one neon/smoked-violet scheme on everything -> Fernando: ugly, "AI". Rebuilt with real materials.
2. Material pass: Damascus v1 was noisy zebra -> layered, warped, two families; stonewash v1 read as granite, v2
   as salt-and-pepper sandpaper -> low-contrast mottle + scuffs; cocobolo v1 blobs, v2 cartoon tiger stripes ->
   thin wavy streaks of varying darkness, continuous grain; mirror v1 soft chrome -> sharp horizon.
3. Geometry pass: balisong slot cut-outs were hidden inside the handle solid -> drawn proud with painted depth;
   back edge, front brake, kicker added; guard given a metallic sheen line (it read as blue plastic).
4. Hand-size pass (~200 px, grass / sky / dark): all three read instantly as different real knives.
Still open (needs the game): first-person scale/pose per knife, Bedrock's own shading of poly_mesh normals, how
RF's atmospheres (Cloud Sea / Novak Glass metal ids) treat the steel, texture filtering at distance.

## Animations, effects, particles, sounds (v6: BUILT - tools/Cosmetics/anims.py + sounds.py, not yet seen in game)
Settings mirror Purxiy's Knives module (docs/PURXIY_MODULES.md): Knife, Inspect key (press again = next trick),
Idle tricks + Trick interval, Animation speed, Draw animation, Trick/attack sounds, Slash arcs, Hit effects.
Bedrock Forge feeds the timing (public Molang vars, src/client/Cosmetics.cpp): your clicks (every swing, hit or
miss), your confirmed hits (GameMode::attack hook), draw, inspect key / idle timer. The pack runs one animation
controller (idle / draw / insp1 / insp2 / swing1 / swing2: swings and tricks alternate) plus hit controllers.

| Knife | Draw | Inspect 1 | Inspect 2 | Attack 1 | Attack 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Karambit | 1.5 turns round the index finger, snaps into the grip (shing + ring ticks + click) | ring roll: lifted, Damascus to the light, two turns, catch | reverse-grip flip and back, both flats shown | hook slash (wind-up, claw leads, ring rolls into it, overshoot, settle) | backhand rip |
| Balisong | real basic opening: safe handle out, twirl, bite handle, latch (clacks + latch) | rollovers round the blade, clack on every stop | aerial toss, catch, bite-handle fan | flick slash, handles rattle | thrust |
| Dagger | toss end over end, caught point-up (low steel ring) | palm twirl, then the mirror blade flashed at you | point-down flip into ice-pick grip and back | stab (pull back, strike, recoil) | slash |

FX (our own particle textures, `textures/bf_cosmetics/fx.png`): slash arcs = soft cool streaks left at the blade tip
along the swing; hit = a spray of hot steel sparks arcing down + a thin cut of light. Gated by the settings.

Sounds (sounds.py, synthesised - nothing sampled): swish x3, cut x2, click x2, ring spin, clack x3, latch, draw shing,
dagger catch. Calibrated against Purxiy's own sound design as reference (its UI sounds: soft, warm, centroid
~1-2.5 kHz, peaks ~0.15-0.35, energy front-loaded). Its knife sounds are not plain files in the loader (they live in
the protected native core), so they were not used; ours follow the same feel.

Offline motion check: `tools/Cosmetics/preview_anim.py` contact sheets (anticipation -> strike -> overshoot -> settle
on every attack). Risks for the in-game pass: first-person particles (attachable particles may spawn at the
third-person hand), controller timing at very high CPS, sound volume vs Hive's own sounds, pose axes.
