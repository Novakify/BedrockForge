# Knives - reference study and design notes (2026-11-17)

Direction (Fernando): each weapon looks like the real thing - shape, construction AND colours/finishes - with our
own details, never 1:1, never a shared "client" colour scheme. Proper, realistic finishes (Damascus, polish, real
handle materials, accessories), not a uniform neon look. Then custom animations, effects and particles per weapon.

## What makes the real ones look real (what we copy in spirit)
- **Damascus (pattern-welded)** is forged with the blade, then acid-etched: the layers run *with* the blade and
  look organic, like flowing water or smoke; a repeating, too-perfect pattern reads as fake/printed. Ladder and
  raindrop come from grooves/holes cut in the billet; twist from twisting the bar. -> our painter: layers along the
  blade face, low-frequency warp + fine lamination, varied layer width, crisp antialiased etch edges; the sharpened
  bevel shows the pattern at low contrast (polished back), never a stamp.
- **Stonewash** = tumbled with abrasive stones: an even matte grey, softly pebbled, scratch-hiding; reads as satin
  from a distance. -> low-contrast mottle + faint curved scuffs, no per-texel sparkle.
- **Satin** = parallel brush lines along the blade; **mirror** = reflects the room: sharp horizon, dark and bright
  bands. -> the dagger's mirror finish has one sharp horizon line, not a soft chrome gradient.
- **Edge**: the cutting edge is ground and sharpened separately from the flats -> every blade gets a bright fresh
  bevel, a crisp apex line and a darker shoulder (`materials.blade_finish`).
- **Karambit anatomy**: curved blade, sharp point, inside (concave) edge, outside edge, ergonomic handle; modern ones
  add the retention ring, a back edge near the tip, front/rear brakes (guards), a thumb rise with jimping.
- **Balisong anatomy**: tang + pivot pins, kicker, choil, swedge (unsharpened top bevel), safe/bite handles, latch
  on the bite handle, zen or tang pins; channel (one milled piece), sandwich (slabs + spacer) or chanwich handles.
- **Fairbairn-Sykes**: slender double-edged spear point with a centre ridge (~7"), short flat guard, vase-shaped
  ring grip (~4.9"), pommel nut; originally often brightly polished.

## Quality audit (offline, honest) - four passes
1. v1-v3 used one neon/smoked-violet scheme on everything -> Fernando: ugly, "AI". Rebuilt with real materials.
2. Material pass: Damascus v1 was noisy zebra -> layered, warped, two families; stonewash v1 read as granite, v2
   as salt-and-pepper sandpaper -> low-contrast mottle + scuffs; cocobolo v1 blobs, v2 cartoon tiger stripes ->
   thin wavy streaks of varying darkness, continuous grain; mirror v1 soft chrome -> sharp horizon.
3. Geometry pass: balisong slot cut-outs were hidden inside the handle solid -> drawn proud with painted depth;
   back edge, front brake, kicker added; guard given a metallic sheen line (it read as blue plastic).
4. Hand-size pass (~200 px, grass / sky / dark): all three read instantly as different real knives.
Still open (needs the game): first-person scale/pose per knife, Bedrock's own shading of poly_mesh normals, how
RF's atmospheres (Cloud Sea / Novak Glass metal ids) treat the steel, texture filtering at distance.

## Animations, effects, particles - proposal for Fernando's greenlight (experimental on purpose)
Everything client side: attachable animations (bones), particles and sounds fired from animation timelines at
locators on the knife, Bedrock Forge timing (draw / inspect / your hits and kills from MatchFx), RF for screen-space
touches. Sounds and particle textures made by us. PvP rule: nothing on the swing that hides the target.

**Karambit**
- Draw: ring spin - one and a half turns round the index finger, ends in a reverse-grip snap; a soft steel "tsing".
- Inspect: the classic roll forward round the ring, catch, then a slow tilt so the Damascus faces the light while a
  specular glint travels down the layers (RF: a moving highlight on the held item's metal id).
- Hit: two or three tiny steel sparks off the hook's tip (locator at the point).
- Experimental: "claw arc" - a hair-thin curved light trace that follows the hook on a crit only.

**Balisong**
- Draw: a real basic opening - both handles start closed round the blade, the safe handle swings out, the knife
  rotates, the bite handle follows, latch click (0.8 s). Needs the handle bones (already in the model).
- Inspect: a short combo (zen rollover -> fan -> aerial catch) with clacks on every handle contact.
- Idle fidget (option): after ~6 s still, a lazy half-flip and back.
- Experimental: the bronze hardware catches a warm flash on each latch click.

**Dagger**
- Draw: toss - it leaves the hand, spins end over end once, caught point-down; a low ring of steel.
- Inspect: commando twirl - spun on the palm round the grip, then held up so the mirror blade flashes the sky.
- Hit: a single bright glint travelling along the ridge.
- Experimental: on your kill, the blade is wiped across the sleeve (a 0.4 s flourish), only you see it.

**All knives (module options)**: inspect key (Fernando picks), auto-inspect on idle, flourish on your kills
(MatchFx), sound volume, per-tier finishes later (e.g. netherite sword = blackwashed Damascus, gold = brass/bronze
hardware) so the sword tier still reads at a glance in a fight.
